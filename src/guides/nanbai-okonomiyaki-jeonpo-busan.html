Nanbai: late-night okonomiyaki and draft beer in Jeonpo

Dinner first, then a café — and then where? In Busan we call the third round of the night 3-cha, and for that we go to Nanbai: a cozy okonomiyaki spot in Jeonpo that stays open until midnight, with great yakisoba, cold draft beer and a really kind owner.

HHyunseo · Busan local for 24 years · Updated November 23, 2026
Quick answer

Nanbai (난바이) in Jeonpo-dong is a late-night okonomiyaki and yakisoba spot that's open until midnight — perfect as the third stop of a date night. Order it spicy (it's not that hot), and always add a topping: pork belly on the okonomiyaki, shrimp on the yakisoba. Okonomiyaki and yakisoba were listed from ₩15,000 (DiningCode, November 2026).

The perfect third stop

Koreans count a night out in rounds: 1-cha (first round), 2-cha, 3-cha. If you're on a date in Jeonpo, here's our plan: dinner for round one, a café for round two, then Nanbai for round three — a hot plate of okonomiyaki and a cold draft beer to end the night.

It's a late-night place that's open until midnight, the atmosphere is great, and the owner is incredibly friendly. That's a big part of why we keep recommending it.

What to order

Okonomiyaki (오코노미야끼) is the house specialty — a savory Japanese pancake cooked on the iron plate and covered in sauce, mayo and bonito flakes. Add pork belly (삼겹살) as a topping. Trust us on this one.

Yakisoba (야키소바) — stir-fried noodles — is just as good, so get one of each if there are two of you. Add shrimp (새우) as the topping.

Go for the spicy flavor. You can choose the flavor, and the spicy one isn't very spicy at all, so it's our pick even if you don't usually like heat.

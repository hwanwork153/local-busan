What we collect

When you send a question through the Ask a Local form, we receive your question, the topic and area you chose, and your email address. The form is processed by a third-party form service, which delivers your message to our inbox.

We use your email only to reply to your question. We never sell or share it. If we publish your question as a guide, we remove your name and email.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to booking sites through affiliate links. Those sites may use cookies to know that you came from Local Busan. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Some pages show banners from our partner Klook. These banners load content from Klook's servers, which may use cookies to measure clicks and bookings.

Fonts and hosting

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may log basic technical information such as your IP address for security.

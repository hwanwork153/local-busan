How the tax refund estimate works

Foreign visitors can get an instant tax refund at participating Olive Young counters on one receipt from ₩15,000 up to under ₩1,000,000 (up to ₩5,000,000 per trip). The refund is roughly 5–8% of the purchase, not the full 10% VAT, so we show a range. Bring your physical passport and show it before you pay.

Tips for better results

Use one product per screenshot, with the name and price visible. Screenshots from the Olive Young app or website read best. If a name comes out garbled, just type over it.

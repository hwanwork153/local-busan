What we collect

When you send a question through the Ask a Local form, we receive your question, the topic and area you chose, and your email address. The form is processed by a third-party form service, which delivers your message to our inbox.

We use your email only to reply to your question. We never sell or share it. If we publish your question as a guide, we remove your name and email.

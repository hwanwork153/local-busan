About Local Busan

We're Minwoo and Hyunseo — two Busan locals who have lived here all our lives.

Local Busan is the guide we wish our friends from abroad had when they visit: the places we actually go to, explained in plain English.

Minwoo Choi

Shopping & practical tips · 27 years in Busan

I've lived in Busan my whole life, and I've lived in a lot of it. My parents have always loved the idea of trying out a new home, so our family moved more than ten times — Dongnae, Dalmaji, Haeundae, Centum and more.

Every move meant learning a new neighborhood from scratch: which gukbap place the neighbors swear by, which café is quiet on a weekday, which bus actually gets you to the beach.

I'm also a big fashion fan and a shopping enthusiast. I love finding the smartest way to do things — where to shop, how to get the best price, how to skip the hassle. I write our shopping guides and practical tips.

Hyunseo

The food expert · 24 years in Busan

I was born and raised in Busan. Whenever I travel or eat somewhere new, I write it down — what I ordered, what was worth it, and the small tips that would have made it even better. Sharing those notes is my favorite part.

Food is where I go deep. I know Busan's restaurants in obsessive detail: the dish that's the real reason to go, the best time to beat the line, and the things nobody tells you until you're already seated. I write our restaurant guides.

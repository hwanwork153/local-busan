Songdo yacht tour: Busan's quieter, more scenic yacht ride

When people think "yacht in Busan", they think Haeundae and Marine City. Most travelers — and plenty of locals — don't know you can also board a yacht right at Songdo Beach. It's less crowded, the coastline is more dramatic, and the ride is a lot of fun.

Minwoo Choi · Busan local for 27 years · Updated November 19, 2026
Quick answer

Skip the crowded yachts at Haeundae and take a yacht tour from Songdo Beach (송도해수욕장) instead. In our experience there are usually only 2–3 groups on one yacht, the cliffs along the Songdo coast beat the Haeundae skyline, and the boat speeds along to K-pop. Daytime tickets were listed from ₩19,900 (child) / ₩25,000 (adult), sunset & night tours from ₩24,900 / ₩29,900 (Naver booking, November 2026).

Why Songdo instead of Haeundae

It's far less crowded. The yacht tours around Haeundae and Marine City are popular, and boats fill up. At Songdo it's a different story: when we went, there were only two or three groups sharing the yacht, so you get space on the deck and room to take photos.

The scenery is better. The Songdo coast has cliffs and rocky headlands you can only see on this side of Busan. We think the views are much better than on the Haeundae side.

It's a party, not a slow cruise. Upbeat K-pop plays while the boat picks up speed. Stand at the back, feel the wind and the spray, and it's pure joy.

Which tour to book

There are two kinds of tickets: a daytime tour (주간) and a sunset & night view tour (선셋&야경). Our photos are from a daytime ride — go in the day for the cliffs and blue water, or pick the sunset slot if you want the evening light.

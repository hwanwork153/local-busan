Olive Young tip: shop on your last day, not your first

Most travelers buy K-beauty the moment they see an Olive Young — then haul heavy bags from Seoul to Busan to Gyeongju. Here's the local way: collect your wishlist as you go, and pick everything up in one stop right before you fly home.

Minwoo Choi · Busan local for 27 years · Updated November 18, 2026
올리브영Travel light
Quick answer

Don't shop on day one. Screenshot every product you like during your trip. On your last day, collect it all at one Olive Young near your hotel or station: either order online and use store pickup (매장 픽업), or turn your screenshots into one list image with our free list maker and show it to staff. Bring your passport for the instant tax refund.

The problem: heavy bags and lost time

Travelers in Korea rarely stay in one city. Skincare, sunscreen and sheet masks add up fast, and carrying them on every train is no fun. Hunting for each product in a busy store also eats hours — shelves are huge, packaging is mostly in Korean, and best-sellers move around.

So we flip it: browse during the trip, buy at the end. You travel with free hands, and your shopping takes minutes instead of an afternoon.

Step 1: Build your wishlist as you travel

Whenever you see something you want — in a store, on social media, in an app — take a screenshot or a photo of the product and its label. Note the shade or size if it matters. That's it. Don't buy yet.

Step 2, option A: Store pickup (매장 픽업)

Olive Young lets you order on its Korean online mall or app and collect the order at a store you choose, with no delivery fee. Here's how it works:

  1. Add your products to the cart in the Olive Young Korea app or website (oliveyoung.co.kr).
  2. At checkout, choose 픽업 (pickup) instead of delivery, and pick the store. Choose one on your way to the station or airport on your last day.
  3. When your order is ready, you get a pickup barcode message.
  4. Show the barcode at the store. You have 3 days to collect it during store hours.

Pickup only works when the store you pick has the products in stock, so place the order a day ahead or on the morning of your last day.

The catch for short-term visitors: the Korean Olive Young app and site need a CJ ONE membership, which asks for Korean identity verification — usually a Korean phone number in your name or an Alien Registration Card. The Olive Young Global mall (the English shop that ships abroad) doesn't offer store pickup. If you live in Korea, study here, or a Korean friend can place the order for you, option A is perfect. If not, use option B.

Step 2, option B: One list image, one store visit

This works for everyone, no account needed.

  1. Open our Olive Young list maker and add your screenshots.
  2. It reads each product name and price for you. Check them, set quantities, and see your total and price after the tax refund. Then save one image — it numbers every product and adds a Korean line asking staff for help.
  3. On your last day, walk into one big Olive Young and show the image to staff. They know the store and can find items far faster than you can.

Don't forget the tax refund

Many Olive Young stores in tourist areas offer an instant tax refund at the counter for foreign visitors: the tax comes off right away, so there's nothing to claim at the airport. Bring your passport and spend over the minimum on a single receipt. Since you're buying everything in one go, your last-day haul is the easiest way to qualify.

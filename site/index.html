Olive Young tip: shop on your last day, not your first

Don't carry Olive Young bags across Korea. How locals do it: save the products you want while you travel, then collect everything in one go before you fly home — with store pickup (매장 픽업) or a one-image shopping list.

Meet your locals

Two Busan natives, 51 years of local know-how between us.

Minwoo Choi

Shopping & practical tips · 27 years in Busan

My parents love trying out new homes, so I grew up moving — more than ten times, all over Busan. Dongnae, Dalmaji, Haeundae, Centum and more. Every move meant a new neighborhood to learn.

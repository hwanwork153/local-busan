Meet your locals

Two Busan natives, 51 years of local know-how between us.

Minwoo Choi

The neighborhood guide · 27 years in Busan

My parents love trying out new homes, so I grew up moving — more than ten times, all over Busan. Dongnae, Dalmaji, Haeundae, Centum and more. Every move meant a new neighborhood to learn: a new gukbap place, a new corner café, a new shortcut to the beach.
